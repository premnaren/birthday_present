  <action>
    1. In `css/finale.css`:
       - Style the handcrafted birthday cake: multi-layered pastel sponge (strawberry & cream colors #F4C2C7 and #FBF3E4), frosting drips, cute sprinkles, and ceramic cake plate.
       - Style 3 birthday candles standing on top with striped wax patterns.

         - Trigger `AudioEngine.playCandleBlow()`.
         - Extinguish flame with smoke puff.
         - Track remaining lit candles.

    USE: Tactile layered SVG/CSS cake with organic flame flickers and tap reactivity.
