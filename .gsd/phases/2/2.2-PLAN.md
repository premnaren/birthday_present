# Plan 2.2: Chapter 1 — "Let Me Guess..." Interactive Guess Cards & Celebration Reveal

## Objective
Implement Chapter 1 ("Let Me Guess...") with a stack of interactive, tactile guessing cards probing Kritika's traits (cozy blankets, painting streaks, cake cravings, solo dance sessions). Provide "Yes" / "Nope" response buttons with witty handwritten reactions, followed by a soft paper confetti burst and a celebratory reveal greeting that unlocks Chapter 2 (Friends).

<task type="auto">
  <name>Build guessing cards UI and interactive Yes/Nope reactions</name>
  <files>
    css/guess.css
    js/guess.js
  </files>
  <action>
    1. In `css/guess.css`:
       - Style the card stack / progressive reveal container.
       - Style each guess card: torn notebook paper backing, washi tape pin at top, handwriting heading (`Caveat`), and large readable body text.
       - Style reaction buttons: cute handwritten paper tags for "Yes! 🙈" and "Nope! 😂" with slight rotation and springy hover effects.
       - Style the reaction stamp: a hand-drawn colored stamp overlay ("KNEW IT! ✨", "DENIAL! 😜", "CLASSIC LUCKY 💖") that stamps onto the card when clicked.
    2. In `js/guess.js`:
       - Load guesses array from `ContentStore.getGuesses()`.
       - Render the cards one by one (or as an interactive step-by-step card deck).
       - When user clicks "Yes" or "Nope":
         - Play subtle tactile sound (`AudioEngine.playStickerPop()`).
         - Stamp the reaction onto the card with a snappy scale-in GSAP animation.
         - Automatically advance to the next card after 1.2 seconds, or provide an "Arrow / Next Guess" button.
    AVOID: Quiz scoring, failure states, countdown timers, or stressful UI.
    USE: Warm, affectionate humor, handwritten stamps, and gentle pacing.
  </action>
  <verify>
    PowerShell:
    `Test-Path css/guess.css; Test-Path js/guess.js`
  </verify>
  <done>
    `css/guess.css` and `js/guess.js` render interactive guessing cards with animated stamp reactions.
  </done>
</task>
